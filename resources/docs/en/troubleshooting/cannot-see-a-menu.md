---
title: I cannot see a menu item
summary: Why an item is missing from the side menu, or a page says forbidden.
keywords: missing menu, forbidden, 403, no access, permission, role, cannot see
order: 2
---
The side menu shows only what your **role** may use, so a missing item usually means your role does not include it.

- Look at the table in [Roles](help:account/roles) to see what each role can do.
- Check your role on the Dashboard or in your profile menu.
- A page that your role does not allow answers "forbidden" even if you type its address.
- If you need more access, ask your school administrator to give you the right role.

Some items appear only when a new part of Somaplus is added to your school's plan.
