---
title: I cannot sign in
summary: The usual reasons sign-in fails and what to do.
keywords: cannot sign in, login fails, wrong password, blocked, too many attempts, wrong address, school not found
order: 1
---
Check these in order.

1. **The address.** Use your own school's Somaplus address. An address that does not belong to a school shows "School not found", and your account does not work at another school's address.
2. **The email address.** Type it exactly as your administrator registered it.
3. **The password.** Check Caps Lock. If you do not remember it, choose **Forgot password?**, see [Forgot your password](help:account/forgot-password).
4. **Too many attempts.** After five wrong passwords in a row, sign-in is blocked for about a minute. Wait and try again.

If it still fails, ask your school administrator to check that your account exists at your school.
