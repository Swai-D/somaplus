When your school's subscription has ended, the school becomes **read-only**. A yellow notice shows on every page.

## What still works

- Signing in and out, and changing the language.
- Looking at everything: academic years, classes, subjects and the Dashboard.

## What does not work

Adding, changing and deleting. Saving a form answers "forbidden".

## What to do

Ask your school administrator to renew the subscription. Once it is renewed, everything works again and nothing is lost. See [Subscription and plan](help:plans/subscription-states).
