**Academics** > **Library** has three pages: the **book list**, the **library members** and **issue and return**. A book is what the school owns; a member is a pupil with a card the school gave them; an issue is one copy that left the shelf and the day it came back.

## Add a book

1. Choose **Add book**. The form opens in a pop-up, so the page is not loaded again.
2. Fill in the **book name**. The subject, the publisher, the author, the number of the book, the rack it stands on and the price are optional.
3. Type how many **copies** the school owns. What is on the shelf is counted from the copies that are out, never typed by hand.
4. **Save book**.

A copy that is out is counted against the book automatically, so the number on the shelf is always true.

## Add a member

Members live under **Library members**. Choose **Add member** and pick a pupil of the school who has no card yet, then give the card its number. A pupil can hold only one card. The phone shown next to a member is the phone of the pupil's guardian, taken from the pupil, never typed here.

The page of a member lists every book the member borrowed and brought back or still has.

## Issue a book and take it back

Under **issue and return**, choose **Issue a book**. Pick the member with their card number, pick a book that still has a copy on the shelf, and pick the day the copy leaves and the day it has to come back (**Return due**). The copy cannot be issued before the pupils have it, a date in the future is refused, and the return day cannot be before the issue day.

The list shows the copies that are still out with their due day, and **Bring back** takes one copy back on the day it is pressed. When a copy's return day has passed and it is still out, the row is marked **Overdue** in red, on the issue list and on the member's page. The filter above the list shows every issue, only the ones out, or only the ones brought back.

## The library card

On the page of a member, **Print library card** opens a printable sheet in a new tab: the school name, the name of the pupil, the card number with a barcode, their class and the year. Use the browser's print for the paper.

## Taking a book or a member out

A book or a member the library ever issued cannot be deleted, because the record of the issues would lose the name it is about: the history of the school is never removed. A book that was never lent, or a member who never borrowed, can be taken out.

## Good to know

- Only the owner, the head teacher and the teachers see the library; the teachers look without changing, and another role can be given the permission in [Custom roles](help:account/custom-roles).
- The subjects a book belongs to are the ones the school has: add them first under [Classes and subjects](help:academics/classes-and-streams).
- The search is sent to the server, so what you type is not kept in the address of the page.
- Every change is written to the audit log.
