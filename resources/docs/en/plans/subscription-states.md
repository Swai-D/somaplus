Your school has a **plan** (Basic, Medium or Enterprise) and a **subscription** with a state. The Dashboard shows both.

## The three states

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Everything in the plan works. |
| **Grace period** | The subscription has lapsed, but everything still works until the date shown on the Dashboard. Ask your administrator to renew. |
| **Read-only** | You can sign in and read all your information, but nothing can be changed until the subscription is renewed. A yellow notice is shown on every page. |

## Your data is safe

A school's data is never deleted because a subscription lapsed. It moves to the grace period and then to read-only, and everything comes back to normal once the subscription is renewed.

See also [Read-only mode](help:troubleshooting/read-only-mode).
