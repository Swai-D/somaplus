## Switch on SMS (owner, once)

Choose **Settings** > **Integrations** > **SMS to parents**, provider **NextSMS (Tanzania)**. Enter your school's NextSMS username, password and the **sender name** (3 to 11 characters, approved by NextSMS), switch on and **Save**. The password is never shown again; leave it empty to keep it. Until this is done, SMS is off and nothing is sent.

## Send reminders (owner and accountant)

1. Choose **Communication** > **Fee reminders**. The page lists invoices that still have a balance. Filter by term or class and choose **Show**.
2. Tick the invoices (or **Select all**). An invoice whose guardians have no valid phone number cannot be ticked; the page says so.
3. Choose the language of the message (English or Swahili) and **Send reminders**.

The page then tells you how many were queued, how many had no valid phone number, how many were already reminded today and how many were already paid.

## What to know

- A guardian gets **at most one reminder per invoice per day**, even if you press the button twice.
- The balance is checked again when each message is sent. If the invoice has been paid in the meantime, the message is cancelled and not sent.
- The message names the school, the student and the balance, and the control number when the invoice has one.
- **Latest reminders** shows each message as Waiting, Sent, Failed or Cancelled, with the reason. Somaplus does not keep the phone number or the text of the message, and the audit log keeps ids only.
- You can send up to 100 invoices at a time. A school in read-only mode can look at the page but cannot send.
