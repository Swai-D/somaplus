## Open the report

Choose **Finance** > **Fees** > **Collections report**. It opens on today; choose **From** and **To** and **Show** to look at any period of up to a year. Owners, head teachers and accountants can open it.

## What it shows

- **Collected**: the money paid in the period (by the date paid, both days included), worked out from the payments themselves. **Reversed** payments are not counted; they are shown on their own so you can see what was undone.
- **By method** (cash, bank, mobile money) and **By source** (typed in by staff, or received from a bank or provider), and **By day**.
- **Still owed on invoices today**: what students still have to pay now, not only in the period. An overpaid invoice does not reduce this.

## Notices that were not recorded

Under **Notices that were not recorded** you see money a bank or provider says it sent but that is not on any invoice (for example the control number was not set yet), or that was refused (for example a different currency). Follow each one up: set the control number on the right invoice, or record the payment by hand. This is how you make sure no money is missed.

The report only reads: it never changes a payment. It shows totals and references, not student names.
