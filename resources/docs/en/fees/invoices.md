## Make invoices

Open a fee structure and, under **Make invoices**, optionally choose a **Stream**, then **Make invoices**. Somaplus makes one invoice for every student placed in that class who is still at school and does not have an invoice from this structure yet.

- Running it again, or clicking twice, never makes a second invoice for the same student. A student who joined the class later simply gets the next invoice.
- Invoices are numbered one after another without gaps (INV-000001, INV-000002 ...), separately for each school.
- The lines and total are copied when the invoice is made. They never change afterwards.

## Find and print an invoice

**Finance** > **Invoices** lists the newest invoices. Choose a term and/or class to narrow the list. Open an invoice to see its lines and **Print** it. Opening an invoice is written to the audit log (ids only).

## Control numbers

On an invoice, the **Control number** is the reference given by the bank or mobile-money channel for paying it. Type 8 to 20 digits and choose **Save**; empty it to clear it. A control number can belong to only one invoice in your school.
