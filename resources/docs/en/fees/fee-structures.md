Open **Finance** > **Invoices** or **Fee structures**. Owners and accountants add and change fee structures; head teachers can look at them.

## Add a fee structure

1. Choose **Add a fee structure**.
2. Type a **Name**, for example `Day scholar` or `Boarding`, then choose the **Term** and the **Class**.
3. Fill in each fee on its own line, for example `Tuition` and its **Amount**. Type amounts like `150000` or `150000.50`. Empty lines are ignored.
4. Choose **Save**.

A class can have several structures in a term as long as their names differ. All amounts are in your school's currency (TZS unless your school uses another one).

## Rules that protect the fees

- An amount must be a plain number above zero with at most two decimals. Anything else is refused and nothing is saved.
- Two fees in the same structure cannot have the same name.
- Once **invoices were made** from a structure it can no longer be changed or deleted, because invoices must keep agreeing with it. Add a new structure instead.
- A term or class that has a fee structure cannot be deleted.
