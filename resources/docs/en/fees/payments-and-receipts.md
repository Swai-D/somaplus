## Record a payment

Open an invoice and, under **Record a payment**, type the **Amount**, choose the **Method** (cash, bank or mobile money), the **Date paid** and, if you have one, a **Reference** such as the bank slip number. Choose **Record payment**. Owners and accountants can record payments.

If the person gives you a **control number**, use **Finance** > **Payments** > **Record a payment by control number**: the payment goes to the invoice that owns that control number.

A receipt is issued at once. Receipts are numbered one after another without gaps (REC-000001, REC-000002 ...), separately for each school, and can be printed.

## Rules that protect the money

- An amount must be a plain number above zero. It cannot be more than what the invoice still owes.
- Sending the same form twice, or clicking twice, never records the money twice.
- A payment is never edited or deleted, and it never changes the invoice. The **Paid**, **Balance** and **Status** of an invoice are worked out from its payments each time.
- Opening a receipt is written to the audit log (ids only).

## Reverse a mistake

An owner can **Reverse** a payment recorded by mistake and must give a reason. The payment and its receipt stay on record (marked as reversed) so numbers never have gaps; the invoice just stops counting the payment, and the money can be recorded again correctly.
