Somaplus gives every school its own address. You work inside your school only.

## Sign in

1. Open your school's Somaplus address in the browser (your administrator gives it to you).
2. Type your email address and password, then choose **Sign in**.
3. The first page you see is the **Dashboard**.

You can only sign in at your own school's address. If you work at two schools, you sign in separately at each one.

> **Note:** After five wrong passwords in a row, sign-in is blocked for about a minute. Wait, then try again. If you forgot your password, see [Forgot your password](help:account/forgot-password).

## Choose your language

Use the flag in the top bar and choose **English** or **Kiswahili**. Menus, messages and the Help centre change at once. Your choice is kept while you use the browser, also after you sign out.

## Sign out

Open your profile in the top bar and choose **Log out**. Always sign out on a shared computer.
