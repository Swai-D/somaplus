Every page has a side menu on the left and a bar along the top. What you see depends on your role.

## The side menu

| Section | Items |
|---|---|
| Main | **Dashboard** |
| Academics | **Students** (**Student list**, **Add student**), **Attendance** (**Take attendance**, **Summary**), **Academic years**, **Classes** (**Class list**, **Subjects**) |
| Support | **Help** |

The menu shows only what your role may use. If an item you expect is missing, see [I cannot see a menu item](help:troubleshooting/cannot-see-a-menu). New items appear here as new parts of Somaplus are added.

## The top bar

- **Flag.** Switch between English and Kiswahili.
- **Full screen icon.** Fills the screen with the page.
- **Your profile.** Shows your name and role, and has **Log out**.

## The Dashboard

The **Dashboard** shows your school's name, its **Plan**, the state of its **Subscription**, the **Academic year** that is current (and the term that today falls in) and **Your role**. A yellow notice at the top of every page means the school is read-only, see [Read-only mode](help:troubleshooting/read-only-mode).
