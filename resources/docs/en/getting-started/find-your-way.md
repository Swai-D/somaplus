Every page has a side menu on the left and a bar along the top. What you see depends on your role.

## The side menu

| Section | Items |
|---|---|
| Main | **Dashboard** |
| Academics | **Students** (**Student list**, **Add student**), **Attendance** (**Take attendance**, **Summary**), **Exams** (**Exams**, **Grading scales**), **Academic years**, **Classes** (**Class list**, **Subjects**) |
| Support | **Help** |

The menu shows only what your role may use. If an item you expect is missing, see [I cannot see a menu item](help:troubleshooting/cannot-see-a-menu). New items appear here as new parts of Somaplus are added.

## The top bar

- **Search.** Finds a student by name or admission number from any page. The search is a POST, so what you type never stays in the address bar.
- **Add new.** The orange button opens the pages you reach for most: add a student, import students from a file, take the attendance, a new exam, a grading scale, a fee structure or a payment. You only see the ones your role may open.
- **Flag.** Switch between English and Kiswahili.
- **Question mark.** Opens the Help centre.
- **Full screen icon.** Fills the screen with the page.
- **Bell.** Tells you what needs your attention: when the subscription of the school ends or the school is read-only, the exams of this year that are still open and the invoices that are not paid in full. A number on the bell says how many notices there are. You only see the notices about a page your role may open.
- **Gear.** Opens the settings of the school (for the owner).
- **Your profile.** Shows your name, your role and your school, and has **Help** and **Log out**.

## The Dashboard

The **Dashboard** opens with coloured tiles for the numbers you are allowed to see: **Students** (those who are active, and how many joined this month), **Guardians**, **Teachers** (the accounts that teach), **Classes**, and for the current academic year the fees **invoiced**, **collected** and **outstanding**. Below them, **Fees by month** compares what was invoiced with what was collected, **Attendance today** shows the share of present, absent, late and excused students, and **People** counts the pupils, the teaching accounts and the staff accounts of the school. Further down, **Attendance of the last 30 days** draws the register of the month, **Students by class** shows how many pupils each class holds in the current year, **Top students** lists the best pupils of the last locked exam with their average and their class position, and **New admissions** and **Recent payments** list the latest five. A block only shows when your role may open its page (students, academics, fees, attendance or results), so a teacher does not see money, an accountant does not see the attendance register, and a parent or student account sees only the **Your school** card. Payments that were reversed are never counted.

The **Dashboard** menu has two pages, as in the template: **School** (the page above) and **Teacher**, for people who enter marks. The Teacher page shows how many students and classes the school has, how many marks you entered, how many exams are still open, today's attendance, and the exams of the current year with a button to **Enter marks** (open exams) or see the **Results** (locked exams).

The **Your school** card shows the school's name, its **Plan**, the state of its **Subscription**, the **Academic year** that is current (and the term that today falls in) and **Your role**. A yellow notice at the top of every page means the school is read-only, see [Read-only mode](help:troubleshooting/read-only-mode).
