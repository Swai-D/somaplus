Every page has a side menu on the left and a bar along the top. What you see depends on your role.

## The side menu

| Section | Items |
|---|---|
| Main | **Dashboard** |
| Academics | **Students** (**Student list**, **Add student**), **Attendance** (**Take attendance**, **Summary**), **Exams** (**Exams**, **Grading scales**), **Academic years**, **Classes** (**Class list**, **Subjects**) |
| Support | **Help** |

The menu shows only what your role may use. If an item you expect is missing, see [I cannot see a menu item](help:troubleshooting/cannot-see-a-menu). New items appear here as new parts of Somaplus are added.

## The top bar

- **Flag.** Switch between English and Kiswahili.
- **Full screen icon.** Fills the screen with the page.
- **Your profile.** Shows your name and role, and has **Log out**.

## The Dashboard

The **Dashboard** opens with coloured tiles for the numbers you are allowed to see: **Students** (those who are active, and how many joined this month), **Guardians**, **Classes**, and for the current academic year the fees **invoiced**, **collected** and **outstanding**. Below them, **Fees by month** compares what was invoiced with what was collected, **Attendance today** shows the share of present, absent, late and excused students, and **New admissions** and **Recent payments** list the latest five. A block only shows when your role may open its page (students, academics, fees or attendance), so a teacher does not see money and an accountant does not see the attendance register. Payments that were reversed are never counted.

The **Dashboard** menu has two pages, as in the template: **School** (the page above) and **Teacher**, for people who enter marks. The Teacher page shows how many students and classes the school has, how many marks you entered, how many exams are still open, today's attendance, and the exams of the current year with a button to **Enter marks** (open exams) or see the **Results** (locked exams).

The **Your school** card shows the school's name, its **Plan**, the state of its **Subscription**, the **Academic year** that is current (and the term that today falls in) and **Your role**. A yellow notice at the top of every page means the school is read-only, see [Read-only mode](help:troubleshooting/read-only-mode).
