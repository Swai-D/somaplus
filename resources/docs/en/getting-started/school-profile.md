## Enter the details (owner)

Choose **Settings** > **School profile**. Fill in the **Phone**, **Email** and **Address** of your school and choose **Save**. Leave a field empty if you do not want it printed.

## Where they appear

Under the school name at the top of every **receipt** and every student **report card**, on screen and when printed. A school that has not filled them in prints only its name.

## Good to know

- The school name itself is set by Somaplus support. Ask them if it must be corrected.
- Only the owner can change the profile. A school in read-only mode can look at it but cannot save.
