**Academics** > **Teachers** lists the teachers of your school. A teacher is a person who signs in to Somaplus, so this page holds both the account and what the school knows about the person: the staff number, the phone, the subjects and the classes.

## Add a teacher

1. Choose **Add teacher**. The form opens in a pop-up, so the page is not loaded again.
2. Fill in the **account**: the full name, the email and a password of at least 8 characters. This is how the person signs in.
3. Fill in the **details**: the phone number, the staff number the school gives them, and optionally the gender, the date of birth, the address, the qualification, the contract type, the shift and the day they joined.
4. Tick the **subjects** the teacher teaches and the **classes** they are in.
5. **Save teacher**.

The teacher now has an account with the teacher role, and their name is in the list. The same email cannot be used twice inside your school, and the staff number has to be free too.

## Look for a teacher

Type the name, the email, the phone or the staff number in the search box and press **Search**. The search is sent to the server, so what you type is not kept in the address of the page.

The filter above the list shows everyone, only the teachers on duty or only those out of duty.

## Change a teacher

Open the teacher and choose **Edit**. Leave the password empty to keep the current one, or type a new one to replace it. The subjects and the classes are replaced with what you tick.

## A teacher who leaves

Choose **Put out of duty**. The teacher keeps the record and the marks they entered, but stops being on duty. **Back in duty** undoes it.

**Take out of the school** removes the teacher for good: the record and what it teaches are deleted. The account stays, so the marks and the attendance the person entered keep their name, and you can give the person another role in [Users](help:account/users).

## Good to know

- Only the owner and the head teacher see this page; a teacher, an accountant and a parent do not.
- The subjects and the classes you tick are the ones the school has: add them first under [Classes and subjects](help:academics/classes-and-streams).
- A teacher with the teacher role can enter marks and take attendance. Their own pages are in [Take attendance](help:attendance/take-attendance).
- Every change to a teacher is written to the audit log.
