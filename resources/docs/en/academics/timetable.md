Open **Academics** > **Timetable**.

## First, the lesson slots

Open **Timetable** > **Lesson slots** and add the slots of your school day, for example `Period 1` from 08:00 to 08:40. Tick **This is a break** for tea or lunch. Slots of one day cannot overlap, and a break cannot hold a lesson. Every class uses the same list.

## Add a lesson

1. Open **Timetable** > **Class timetable**, choose the **Term** and the **Class**, and choose **Show**.
2. Choose **Add a lesson**.
3. Choose the subject, the day, the slot, and optionally a stream, a teacher and a room. Leave the stream empty for a lesson given to the whole class.
4. Choose **Save**.

Somaplus refuses a lesson when:

- the class (or the stream) already has a lesson in that slot. A lesson for the whole class clashes with every stream, and the other way round;
- the teacher already teaches somewhere else in that slot;
- the room is already used in that slot;
- the teacher is out of duty, or the subject or class is outside what the teacher was assigned under **Teachers**. A teacher with nothing assigned yet is not restricted.

The message says which of these it is, so you can fix it.

## Look at a timetable

- **Class timetable** shows a class, or one stream of it, with the teacher of each lesson.
- **Teacher timetable** shows one teacher's week, with the class of each lesson.
- **My timetable** shows a teacher their own week.
- Choose **Print** to print a timetable or save it as a PDF.
- A parent sees the timetable of each of their children under **My children**.

Saturday and Sunday only appear when they hold a lesson.

## A new term

Choose **Copy a term**, pick the term to copy from and the term to copy into. Lessons that do not fit are skipped and counted. Running it twice adds nothing the second time.

## Good to know

- A timetable belongs to a term, so past terms keep theirs.
- If a teacher is removed from the school, their lessons stay on the timetable with **No teacher yet**.
- A term, class, stream, subject or slot that still holds lessons cannot be deleted.
- A school that is read-only can still look at timetables but cannot change them.
