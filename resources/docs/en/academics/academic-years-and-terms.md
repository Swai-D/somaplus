Open **Academics** > **Academic years**. Owners and head teachers can add and change; teachers and accountants can only look.

## Add an academic year

1. Type the **Name** (for example `2026`), the start date and the end date.
2. Tick **This is the current academic year** if it is the one now in progress. Only one year is current: choosing another replaces the previous one.
3. Choose **Save**.

Years of one school cannot overlap, and names must be different. The Dashboard shows the current year.

## Add terms

1. Open the year and fill in **Add a term**: the term name (for example `Term 1`) and its dates.
2. Choose **Save**.

A term must lie inside its academic year, and terms of one year cannot overlap. When today falls inside a term, the Dashboard shows that term.

## Change or delete

- You cannot shorten a year so that one of its terms falls outside it. Change the term first.
- A year that still has terms cannot be deleted. Delete its terms first.
- When the school is read-only you can look but not change, see [Read-only mode](help:troubleshooting/read-only-mode).
