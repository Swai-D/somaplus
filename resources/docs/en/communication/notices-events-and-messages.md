## Where to find it

Choose **Communication** in the sidebar. The section has the **Notice board**, the **Events** calendar, **Messages** and **Fee reminders**. Everybody who can see the section can read everything in it; putting a notice or an event needs the roles that may manage communication (by default owner and head teacher).

## Notice board

1. Choose **Communication** > **Notices**. The board lists its notices with the day of each.
2. Choose **Put up a notice**, give the day, the title and (optionally) a description, and save. The notice appears with the day it speaks from.
3. Choose **Edit** next to a notice to change it or take it off the board.

## Calendar of events

1. Choose **Communication** > **Events**. The month shows as a grid; events appear on the days they run. Use the arrows to move between months.
2. Choose **Add event**, give when it starts, when it ends (leave the end empty for an all-day event) and what it is, and save. The calendar opens on the month of the event.
3. **Coming up**, at the side, lists the next ten events: those still running come first.
4. Choose an event to change it or take it off the calendar.

## Messages

Choose **Communication** > **Messages**. Every message the school sent through its SMS gateway is listed, the most recent first: the day, the type (fee reminder), the student and the guardian, and how it went: **Waiting**, **Sent**, **Failed** or **Cancelled**, with the reason when it failed. Filter by status or type. Only the latest 200 are shown.

Somaplus keeps no phone numbers and no message texts here: both are worked out again when a message is sent, so what failed is recorded but what was typed is not.

## Fee reminders

**Communication** > **Fee reminders** opens the page for sending fee reminders by SMS; see the guide *Remind parents of fees by SMS*.
