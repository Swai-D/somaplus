Results are shown only for a **locked** exam, because only final marks count. Open the exam and choose **Open the results**, then pick the **Class** and, if you want, a **Stream**. Owners, head teachers and teachers can look at results.

## How the numbers are worked out

- **Total** is the sum of the student's scores in the exam.
- **Average** is the total divided by the number of subjects with a score. A subject where the student was absent, or has no score, is not counted. A score of `0` counts.
- **Grade** comes from the average and the exam's own grading scale. An average of 79.995 is **not** rounded up to 80: it stays in the lower grade.
- **Position in class** is by average. Students with exactly the same average share a position and the next position is skipped (1, 1, 3). **Position in stream** is the same inside the stream. Choosing one stream still shows the position in the whole class.
- A student with no score has no average and no position and is listed last.

The class used is the one recorded when the marks were entered, so results of a past term stay the same even if the student moves class later.

## Report card

On a student's page, **Report cards** lists the terms with a locked exam that has marks for the student. The card shows every locked exam of the term: the score and grade of each subject, the total, average, overall grade and positions. Use **Print** to print it. A term total across several exams is not worked out, because schools weigh exams differently.

Every time results or a report card are opened, it is written to the audit log (ids only). Accountants and parent/student accounts cannot open them.
