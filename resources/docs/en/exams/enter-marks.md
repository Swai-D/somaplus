Open an exam and, under **Enter or look at marks**, choose the **Class**, optionally the **Stream**, and the **Subject**, then **Show**. Owners, head teachers and teachers can enter marks.

## Enter the marks

1. Type each student's **Score** (out of the exam's maximum). Use a point or a comma for decimals, with at most two decimals, for example `45` or `45.5`.
2. For a student who did not sit the exam, tick **Absent** and leave the score empty.
3. Choose **Save marks**.

The list holds the students placed in that class for the exam's academic year, and not students who have left school. The **Grade** is worked out by Somaplus from the exam's grading scale after you save; it cannot be typed.

## Rules that protect the marks

- A score must be a plain number from 0 up to the exam's maximum. Anything else is refused and **nothing** on the page is saved until every row is correct.
- A student cannot be absent and have a score.
- An empty score keeps the mark that is already stored. `0` is a real score.
- Sending the same page again, or saving a correction, changes the stored marks and never makes duplicates.
- Every save is written to the audit log.

## When marks cannot be entered

If the exam is **locked**, or the school is read-only, the marks can be looked at but not changed, see [Exams and locking](help:exams/exams-and-locking).
