Open **Academics** > **Exams** > **Exams**. Owners and head teachers add and change exams; teachers can look and enter marks.

## Add an exam

1. Choose **Add an exam**.
2. Type the **Name** (for example `Midterm`), choose the **Term**, the **Grading scale** and what the exam is **Out of** (for example 100 or 50).
3. Choose **Save**.

Exam names must be different within a term. The academic year is taken from the term. A grading scale must exist first, see [Grading scales](help:exams/grading-scales).

## Once marks are entered

The term, the maximum mark and the grading scale of an exam can no longer change, so that the grades stay the same. The name can still be changed. An exam with marks cannot be deleted, and neither can a year, term or subject that has exams or marks.

## Lock the exam

When the marks are final, open the exam and choose **Lock exam**. A locked exam accepts no new marks and no changes, for anyone, and cannot be edited or deleted. Only an owner or head teacher can **Unlock** it again. Locking and unlocking are written to the audit log.
