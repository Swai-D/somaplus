---
Open **Academics** > **Attendance** > **Summary**.

1. Choose the **Class**, optionally a **Stream**, and the dates **From** and **To** (the default is the start of this month until today).
2. Choose **Show**.

Each student has a count of **Present**, **Absent**, **Late** and **Excused** days, and the **Attendance rate**: present and late days as a percentage of present, late and absent days. Excused days are left out of the rate. Students with no marks in these dates are not listed.
