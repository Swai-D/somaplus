Open **Academics** > **Attendance** > **Take attendance**. Owners, head teachers and teachers can take attendance.

## Mark a class

1. Choose the **Class**, optionally a **Stream**, and the **Date** (today is chosen for you), then choose **Show**.
2. For each student choose **Present**, **Absent**, **Late** or **Excused**. Everyone starts as present. Type a **Note** if useful, for example "Sick".
3. Choose **Save attendance**.

The list holds the students who are in that class on that day: the class of their newest placement in that academic year, and not students who have left school. If it is empty, place students in the class first, see [Class history and promotion](help:students/classes-and-promotion).

## Correct a register

Open the same class and date, change the marks and save again. Saving a day again updates it; it never makes duplicates. A date in the future, or a date outside every academic year, is refused.

## Old registers stay as they were

Each mark remembers the class the student was in that day, so moving a student to another class later does not change earlier registers.
