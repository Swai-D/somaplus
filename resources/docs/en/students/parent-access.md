## Give access (owner)

1. Open **Guardians** and choose the guardian. The guardian needs an **email**: add it first if it is empty.
2. In **Access to the family pages** choose **Give access**.
3. The guardian receives an email with a link to choose a password. If the email cannot be sent, tell them to use **Forgot password** on your school's address.

An email that already belongs to another account of your school cannot be used.

## What the parent sees

Only their own children, the ones linked to that guardian: the fee invoices (total, paid, balance, control number), the payments received with their receipt numbers, and the report card of each term. Report cards show only exams you have finished and locked. A parent cannot see any other child, any school page, or change anything.

## Take access away

Open the guardian and choose **Take access away**. The account loses its role and its password, any sign-in on other devices ends, and nothing is deleted. A guardian who has access cannot be deleted until it is taken away. If a child is unlinked from the guardian, the parent stops seeing that child at once.
