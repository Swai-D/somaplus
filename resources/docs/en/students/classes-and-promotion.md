Every student has a **Class history** on the student page.

## Place or move a student

1. Open the student.
2. Under **Place in a class**, choose the **Academic year**, **Class** and **Stream** and the date.
3. Choose **Save**.

Use the same form to **promote** a student to the next academic year or to **move** the student to another class or stream.

## History is never overwritten

Placing a student adds a new line to the history; earlier lines stay exactly as they were, so reports for a past term can always be reproduced. For one academic year, the newest line is the one that counts. Sending the same placement twice adds nothing.

## Good to know

- A student who has left school must be brought back before being placed, see [Students who leave school](help:students/leaving-school).
- A class, stream or academic year that has students placed in it cannot be deleted.
