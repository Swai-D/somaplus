A student's record is never deleted. When a student leaves, mark it.

## Mark as left

1. Open the student.
2. Under **Mark as left school**, type the date and, if you wish, a reason.
3. Choose **Mark as left school**.

The student leaves the active list and can be found under **Left school** in the student list. The record, guardians and class history stay.

## Bring back

Open the student and choose **Bring back to school**. The student is active again and can be placed in a class.
