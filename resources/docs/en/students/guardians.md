A parent or a guardian can be added from a student's page, or on their own under **Academics** > **Guardians**.

## The guardian list

**Academics** > **Guardians** has the two subsections of the template: **Add New Guardian** and **Guardians List**.

**Guardians List** shows every parent and guardian of the school with their pupils, their phone number, their email address and the day they were added. The table is a datatable: its own search box sits in the header of the card, with the number of rows per page, the sorting of the columns and the paging, as in the template.

**Add New Guardian** opens a small pop-up with the name, the phone number and the email address; the page is not loaded again. The pencil of a row opens the same pop-up filled with that guardian, and a phone number that is already in the school is refused with the pop-up opening again and what you typed kept.

## The page of one guardian

The page of a guardian shows their details and the pupils they are the parent of, with the class of each pupil. Under **Link a pupil** you add a pupil to the guardian and say how they are related (father, mother, guardian or other); a pupil can have one **main contact**, and choosing another replaces the previous one. **Take off** unlinks a pupil from the guardian.

## Add a parent or guardian to a student

Open the student and go to **Parents and guardians**.

1. Type the **Name** and **Phone**, and optionally the **Email**.
2. Choose the **Relationship** (father, mother, guardian or other).
3. Tick **Main contact** if this person is the first to call.
4. Choose **Save**.

## Brothers and sisters

A school keeps one record for each phone number. When you add the same phone number to a second child, Somaplus links the existing parent instead of making a copy, and keeps the name and email already saved. A phone number that is already in the school is refused when you add a guardian on their own.

## Remove

**Remove** unlinks the parent from this student. A parent who is left with no student at the school is deleted completely, because data nobody needs is not kept. A guardian with a pupil is never deleted: take the pupil off first, then remove the guardian.
