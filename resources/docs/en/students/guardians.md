Open the student and go to **Parents and guardians**.

## Add a parent or guardian

1. Type the **Name** and **Phone**, and optionally the **Email**.
2. Choose the **Relationship** (father, mother, guardian or other).
3. Tick **Main contact** if this person is the first to call. A student has one main contact: choosing another replaces the previous one.
4. Choose **Save**.

## Brothers and sisters

A school keeps one record for each phone number. When you add the same phone number to a second child, Somaplus links the existing parent instead of making a copy, and keeps the name and email already saved.

## Remove

**Remove** unlinks the parent from this student. A parent who is left with no student at the school is deleted completely, because data nobody needs is not kept.
