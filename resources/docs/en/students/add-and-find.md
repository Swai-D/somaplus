Open **Academics** > **Students** > **Student list**. Owners and head teachers can add and change; teachers and accountants can only look.

## Add a student

1. Choose **Add student** (or **Academics** > **Students** > **Add student**).
2. Type the **Admission number**, the names, the gender, the date of birth and the date admitted.
3. Optionally choose the **Academic year**, **Class** and **Stream** to place the student at once.
4. Choose **Save**.

Admission numbers must be different within your school. Collect only what is needed: Somaplus asks for no more than this.

## Find a student

- **Search** by name or admission number. At least two letters are needed. What you type is sent safely and is not kept in the address of the page.
- **Filter** the list by class for the current academic year, or switch to students who **Left school**. A current academic year must be set, see [Academic years and terms](help:academics/academic-years-and-terms).

## Change a student

Open the student and choose **Edit**. Every time a student's record is opened or changed, Somaplus writes a line in the school's audit log.
