Owners and head teachers can print ID cards. A card is printed from the data you already have: nothing extra to type.

## One student

Open the student and choose **Print ID card**. The card opens in a new tab.

## A class or several students

1. Open **Academics** > **Students** > **Print ID cards**.
2. Choose a class (or **All classes**). The active students of the current academic year are listed, all ticked.
3. Untick anyone you do not want, then **Print selected cards**. Up to 200 cards are printed at a time.

## Printing

On the card page choose **Print**. Use **A4**, scale **100%** (not "fit to page"), and turn on **background graphics** if your browser asks. Eight cards fit on a page, in the standard bank-card size (85.6 × 54 mm); cut along the borders and laminate.

## What is on the card

The school name, the student's name, admission number, class and stream of the current academic year, the year, and a barcode of the admission number that a scanner can read later. Only what a card needs is used: no date of birth and no parent details. Somaplus does not collect photos of children, so the card shows the student's initials.

Students who have left are not printed. Every card printed is written to the audit log. Cards can be printed even when the school is read-only.

## Tips

- *No class on the card*: the student has no placement in the current academic year. Place them first (see [Add and find students](help:students/add-and-find)).
- *Wrong year on the card*: mark the right year as current under **Academic years**.
- The whole path from admission to card is in [From admission to ID card](help:students/admission-to-id-card).
