You can choose a new password yourself, by email.

## Ask for a link

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password?**.
2. Type the email address you use at your school and choose **Send reset link**.

For safety, the page gives the same answer whether or not the address belongs to your school. If it does, a link arrives by email.

## Choose the new password

1. Open the link in the email.
2. Type a new password of at least 8 characters, type it again to confirm, and choose **Save new password**.
3. Sign in with the new password.

The link works for 60 minutes and only once. It works only at the school that sent it: if you work at two schools, ask for a link at each school's own address.

## If no email arrives

Check your spam folder, wait a minute and try again, or ask your school administrator.
