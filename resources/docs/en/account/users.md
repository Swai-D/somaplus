**Settings** > **Users** lists everyone who signs in to your school, with their role. Only the **owner** sees this page. It is available on every plan.

## Add a person

1. Choose **Add a user**.
2. Type the name and email, choose the **role** and set a **password** of at least 8 characters.
3. **Save**, then give the email and password to the person safely. They sign in at your school address and can change the password later.

The same email can belong to a person at another school; inside your school an email is used once.

## Change a person

Choose **Edit** to change the name, email or role. Leave the password empty to keep it, or type a new one to replace it. A change of role is written to the audit log.

## Good to know

- A person has one role. Roles are explained in [Roles](help:account/roles) and your own in [Custom roles](help:account/custom-roles).
- The school always keeps at least one owner: you cannot take the owner role from the last owner.
- A person's pages and menu follow their role at once.
