Every school has the same five roles: **Owner**, **Head teacher**, **Teacher**, **Accountant** and **Parent / student**.

## What a role decides

A role decides what a person can see and do. The side menu shows only what the person's role allows, and a page that the role does not allow answers "forbidden" even if someone types its address.

| What | Owner | Head teacher | Teacher | Accountant | Parent / student |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Look at academic years, classes and subjects | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add, change and delete them | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Look at students, parents and class history | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add and change students, parents and placements | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Take and look at attendance | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Look at exams, grading scales and marks, and enter marks | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Look at results, positions and report cards | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Look at the library: books, members and the copies out | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Add books and members, put books out and take them back | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Look at fee structures and invoices | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | No |
| Change fee structures, make invoices, set control numbers | Yes | No | No | Yes | No |
| Record payments and issue receipts | Yes | No | No | Yes | No |
| Reverse a payment | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Send fee reminders by SMS | Yes | No | No | Yes | No |
| Set up integrations (Settings > Integrations) | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Give a guardian access to the family pages | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Open the family pages: own children's fees and results | No | No | No | No | Yes |
| Change the school profile (Settings > School profile) | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Add and change grading scales and exams, lock and unlock exams | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |

More rows are added here as new parts of Somaplus arrive.

## Your own roles

On the **Enterprise** plan the owner can make **custom roles** next to these five, with exactly the permissions the school wants. See [Custom roles](help:account/custom-roles). Anyone can be given a role under **Settings** > **Users** ([Users](help:account/users)).

## Good to know

- A person's role belongs to one school. The same person at another school needs a role there too.
- Your role is shown on the Dashboard and in your profile menu.
