The five default roles (Owner, Head teacher, Teacher, Accountant, Parent / student) fit most schools. When a person needs something in between, for example a bursar who also looks at attendance, make a **custom role**. Custom roles are part of the **Enterprise** plan and only the **owner** can make them.

## Make a role

1. Open **Settings** > **Roles** and choose **Add a role**.
2. Type a name (for example *Registrar*). The name cannot be the name of a default role.
3. Tick what the role can do. The permissions are grouped by area (Students, Fees, Exams and so on). Choosing one also chooses the permissions it needs to work, for example changing students includes looking at them.
4. To start from an existing role, choose it under **Start from the permissions of** and adjust.
5. **Save**.

## Give the role to people

Open **Settings** > **Users**, add a person or edit one, and choose the role ([Users](help:account/users)). The menu and pages of that person follow the role at once.

## Change or delete a role

- **Edit** a custom role to change its name or permissions. The change applies to everyone who has it, immediately.
- **Delete** works only when nobody has the role. Give those people another role first.
- The five default roles cannot be changed or deleted.

## Good to know

- Managing settings, users and roles stays with the owner and cannot be given to a custom role.
- If the plan later drops below Enterprise, people keep the custom roles they already have, but the roles page is closed until the plan is Enterprise again.
- Every change to a role is written to the audit log.
