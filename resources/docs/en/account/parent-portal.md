## Sign in

The school office gives you access and you receive an email with a link to choose a password. Then sign in with your email on the school's address. If you lose the password, use **Forgot password**.

## My children

After signing in you see your children. Under each name you see whether anything is owed. Choose **Open** to see:

- **Results**: choose a term to see the report card. Only exams the school has finished and locked appear.
- **Fee invoices**: the total, what has been paid, the balance and the control number to pay with.
- **Payments received**, with the receipt number.

You can only look; you cannot change anything. If a child is missing, ask the school office to link them to your record.
